About

Analytical, but not boring

I turn internal and external datasets into data-driven insight using SQL, Python, Tableau, and Excel. My two main projects — an e-commerce customer behavior analysis and a hotel booking demand analysis — take raw data through cleaning, feature engineering, and full statistical analysis to answer real business questions. Beyond those, I've built five more EDA projects covering used cars, video games, and more.

I'm comfortable maintaining clean datasets end to end: load and explore, clean and transform, analyze with Pandas and NumPy, then build dashboards and visual summaries that a business audience can act on. I'm a Business Studies student at Tribhuvan University, and this portfolio is my first step into the field — every project here is real work, not a template.
